# Engagement rules: apply to ALL future Seldom films (from film 4 onward)

The user found the first films (Mary Celeste, Antwerp, Mummy) boring. Every future film must follow these rules.

1. **Pacing:** cut every 1.5–3 s (about double the old shot count). Land cuts on VO phrases and emphasis words.
2. **Motion in every shot:** no static or slow drifts. Use fast push-ins, crane reveals, whip pans, orbits, rack focus and dolly-zooms on reveals. People must DO things. Animate props.
3. **Zoufry-style explainer shots:** clean high top-down 3D dioramas on a dark blue-grey background (never pitch black), with the key object highlighted in Seldom yellow #E3FF00 (lit faces saturated, olive shadows). Use them whenever the VO explains something. Include stylised 3D map shots with yellow pins.
4. **Variety:** alternate cinematic shots, top-down explainers, extreme macros and maps. Never three similar shots in a row.
5. **Hook:** the first 10 s is the strongest moment of the film: rapid cuts of the central mystery, ending on a hard stop.
6. **Music:** a dark documentary score bed (royalty-free / CC0) for each part, mixed with the SFX, with drops and silences at reveals.
7. **Graphics list per part:** CapCut overlays for the user (timecode, arrow or label text, target, dates and numbers).
8. **Thumbnails:** the same dark premium style. Hero object in Seldom yellow, everything else dark monochrome blue-grey, soft top spotlight, space for an arrow and text, no text in the render.

All earlier rules still apply: FARM_DELIVERY_RULES.md; no face close-ups; no crossed hands; 0 clips; natural clothing; no burned-in cards or captions.
