## Our own work
The following were made procedurally in `../FrozenTearsGun_v001/scripts/`:
- the tear gun (brass, glass, valve wheel, CO2 canister, spring, plunger, trigger)
- the monocle funnel and silicone tube
- the tears, ice pellets, frost and gas
- the thought bubble, explainer backdrop and sparks
- the office window, blinds and monitor
- the workshop pegboard (with procedural holes), shelves, panelled door and blueprint-grid posters
- the lathed CO2 canister, with a domed base, shoulder, threaded neck and brushed wear

Props with printed text or logos were removed: the spray cans, measuring tape, stapler, wall clock, binder and stationery set. The T-shirt graphic was also painted out of the clothing texture.
