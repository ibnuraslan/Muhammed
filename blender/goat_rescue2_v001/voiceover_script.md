Full text:

A charging cow threw him into a heap of manure, breaking his hip. Stuck and unable to move, he had no way to call anyone. For five days he lay trapped in the muck, scorched by the sun and shivering through the night. But someone found him. His goat tracked him down and refused to leave. At night, she lay pressed against him to keep him warm. With no food or water, he grew weaker by the day. So she stood still while he milked her into an old bottle, just enough to stay alive. Rescuers finally found him and rushed him to hospital. And he says he'd never have survived without her.
