## Capture, f1726–1800: the goat read as airborne
**What was measured.** The goat's evaluated mesh was checked against the ground for f1700–1810. The goat was never off the ground:
- Lowest point 0.01 m, highest 0.78–0.83 m, so it is standing.
- Deform hooves at 0.00–0.06 m.
- Torso level throughout.

**Why it still looked like a flying goat.** The camera at f1735 sat 4 m up, looking steeply down. From that angle a walking stride reads as a leap, and the low sun throws the shadow well away from the feet. At f1786–1796 the camera looked down its back, which read as hanging vertically on the pole.

**Re-staged.**
- The camera is at ground level, side-on, for f1735–1800 (keys 1735, 1770 and 1800, tracker `ga`). The goat walks in profile with a normal four-beat gait and its hooves visibly on the ground in every frame.
- The officer holds the catch-pole with both hands, the loop over the goat's neck. He leads it to the ramp and up it; there is no lifting.
- The official in blue no longer crosses the shot. He follows on the far side, 2 m or more from the goat. He stops at f1760, then walks off behind the camera, out of view and at least 2 m from it.
