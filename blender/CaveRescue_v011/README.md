## The film
- **Jungle cave mouth (0–5.7 s):** the coach and four boys in jerseys walk into an irregular limestone mouth. The coach and two boys carry lanterns gripped on the top handle.
- **Flood (5.7–9.5 s):** rain falls outside, water slides in across the floor, and they look back and run deeper.
- **Cutaway (9.5–14.8 s):** the front half of the carved mountain drops away (instant, inside the move), showing a strata cross-section. A glowing route line grows to the chamber, the sumps fill and the line turns blue, and a red beacon marks the boys.
- **Chamber (14.8–17.7 s):** the camera flies into the cut, and the front closes behind the lens. The boys sit on the mud ledge, and a diver surfaces in the pool with his lamp on them.
- **Flooded gap and lost diver (17.7–22 s):** divers swim the sump along the yellow guide line. A lone diver swims away and his lamp fades out. No body is shown.
- **Rescue (22–31.4 s):** two divers kneel with a spare tank and the logo gear bag. The injection is into the boy's left deltoid, and a full-face mask is brought to his face and strapped.
- **Tow (31.4–35 s):** the sedated boy, in an orange SKED stretcher with bubbles from his mask, is towed under water. The cutaway then shows lights moving one by one along the route.
- **Out (35–40.6 s):** the stretcher is carried out of the mouth between the cheering coach and boys. The ambulance has the DA KRAS FILMS logo, a medic cheers, and the camera cranes up.
