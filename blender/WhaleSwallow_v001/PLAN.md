# B02 WhaleSwallow: plan (Phase 1, no VO yet)

ConnectB02. Branch `claude/connectB02-whale-swallow`. 1080×1920, 60 fps, Cycles CPU, hard cuts.
Reference: `briefs/batch2/B02_WhaleSwallow_reference_1fps.jpg` (36 s). Per the Nov 15 client direction we **match its
shot order, subjects, framing ideas and pacing**, rebuilt in our look (bright 3D animation, real models, no text).
The beat times below are the reference's seconds. They become VO frames in Phase 2 (`1 + round(s × 60)`).
Every clip is animated at 1× and about 30% longer than its slot, so it can be trimmed to the VO, never stretched.

## Cast and sets
- **Diver** (hero): middle-aged man, generic MPFB face (DiverC from CaveRescue, re-dressed). Bare chest, **red swim
  trunks** (as in the reference), barefoot. Eye fix, catchlight, elbow limit opened, relaxed hands.
  In hospital: an **orange life vest** (real CC-BY model, fitted and bound to the spine) over the bare chest, with
  clean stylised purple bruises on the cheek, chest and left knee (no wounds, no blood).
- **Doctors**: two MPFB vets from ToucanBeak (a man in blue scrubs and a woman in green scrubs, gloves removed).
- **Humpback whale**: real CC-BY model (K9239, 51k faces) with our own rig: spine and tail chain, flippers and a
  **jaw bone** for the lower jaw, plus throat-pleat swell. One whale, recoloured to clean slate-grey and white.
- **Great white shark** (imagined beat only): real CC-BY rigged model (LasquetiSpice) with its bite cycle.
- **Sea surface**: Ocean modifier, bright blue sky with soft clouds. **Underwater**: bright cyan-to-teal gradient
  with light rays and plankton specks, readable, never murky.
- **Mouth interior set**: a full-scale built cavity, not the whale model's inside: a pink, glossy tongue floor, a
  domed palate, **rows of cream-grey baleen plates** hanging down both sides, a gap between the lips that lets
  bright sea-blue light in, and water sloshing on the tongue. Warm pink fill, so it's never a black frame.
- **Hospital room**: a white hospital bed (real CC-BY model), chequered floor and pale-blue walls as in the
  reference, an IV stand, a wall cabinet and a monitor with a blank screen.
- **Logo**: (1) white DA KRAS FILMS logo on the **orange life vest's front panel** (where the reference vest carries a
  label), shots 9a and 9c. (2) dark logo on a **poster on the hospital wall** behind the doctors, shot 10.
  Neither is in the knee shot or on skin.

## Risky shots and the simple, safe staging
1. **Inside the mouth (2, 5a–c)**: a built set, lit pink and bright with a sea-blue light gap; never dark. The diver's hands go flat on the baleen plates (`place_hand` on the plate surface), checked with `body_prop_cross` against the plates and tongue. No camera within 1 m of the plates.
2. **Diver in the breach (1a–b)**: he's a body in the gap between the jaws with only his head, shoulders and arms out, the hands pressed flat on the upper jaw. We cut before the jaws meet: no crushing.
3. **Spit-out arc (6b)**: wide, side-on, the man about 1/8 of the frame height. A keyed root arc, with a tucked pose that rotates about 120°. No limb near the jaws. It splashes into the sea with a foam ring.
4. **Shark beat (3a–c)**: imagined, so it's quick. The jaws stay open; no tooth touches him. The diver is small in the mouth.
5. **Knee on the bed (8a–b)**: the leg rests on the mattress (contacts checked against the mattress top), the hand on the thigh above the knee, f/11, nothing in the lens. The bruise is a clean purple patch.
6. **Life vest (7–10)**: fitted to the torso with Shrinkwrap 6 mm, then weights transferred from the body. Checked for arm-through-vest in every hospital shot (arms hang outside it).
7. **Doctors (10)**: no arm crosses a face; the arms are crossed on their own chests or hang. Clean scrub colours (blue, green).
