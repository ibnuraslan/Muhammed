## Changes made to CC-BY models
- **Car:** scaled and rotated, roof removed, repainted. Brass lamps are emissive, and a brass grab rail was added. The model has no badges or text.
- **Lungs:** scaled to fit the character's thorax, with a breathing scale animation.
- **Skeleton:** only the rib cage is used, scaled to the thorax and given a clean bone material.
- **Clock:** scaled, with new animated hands added.
- **Lamp:** used as hotel wall lanterns and as the heads of the gas street lamps.
- **Facade scans:** scaled, and decimated to 30 %. A lit interior with warm windows sits behind the open frames. The scans are used as the hotel and the side buildings.
- **Carriage:** scaled and parked at the kerb.
