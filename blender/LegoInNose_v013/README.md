## The final round (after the client watched check 3)
The client approved one last round with three changes, and nothing else changed:
1. **The boy's insertion hand.**
   - His arm is relaxed and his elbow down.
   - The hand comes up from below his left side, the fingers pointing up along the nostril.
   - The thumb and index fingertips pinch the brick's outer end.
   - No fist is raised in front of his face, for the red brick or the yellow one.
2. **Mom's tweezer removal, restaged.**
   - She moves from the sofa while the camera is close on the boy's face, then kneels on the rug at his front-left.
   - The boy turns three-quarters to her with his head tilted up.
   - The tweezers, in her right hand, enter in front of his face.
   - Her hand and fingers stay at least 11 mm clear of his face, hair, brows and lashes (audited every 2nd frame, 800–1198).
   - The yellow brick comes out on the tweezer tip, and she lifts it up between their faces for the camera.
3. **The colour grade.**
   - The view is Filmic High Contrast, which keeps the LEGO red true red.
   - The sets are deep and saturated: teal living room, purple sofa, royal-blue rug, saturated blue bedroom, teal and purple bathroom tiles, and a purple-to-blue void.
   - Skin and clothes are warm: orange stripes, magenta and amber.
   - The bricks are a true red and a pure yellow.
   - The x-ray window is dark teal and in focus (f/22), with the brick glowing slightly while it is inside a head.
   - Fixed along the way: the bathroom tile texture was rendering as white mortar (the old "milky" haze), and the sofa's black zebra came from duplicate surfaces. The camera catchlight now scales with distance, so close-ups no longer blow out.
