# BellyFat: changes from checkpoint B to C

## Camera
- One solver for the whole film. The target is a smoothed bullet-tip reference plus Catmull-Rom offsets; the position is spherical (az, el, log distance) around it.
  - This removed the velocity kinks at anchor changes, including a 4× drop at f116 and the near-stops at 0.3 s and 0.8 s.
- New opening. A slow dolly on the woman (0.55 → 1 m/s) is cross-blended into the bullet-locked chase from 0.3 to 0.75 s; that blend is the whip. Before, the camera started at 12 m/s.

- Depth of field keyed on the target: f/2.8 in the store, rising to f/11 at macro scale. Motion blur is on for the farm (shutter 0.5).
