## People, clothes, hair

- MakeHuman / MPFB2 system assets (CC0): bodies, skins, eyes, hair (short02, short04, long01, braid01), eyebrows, eyelashes, teeth, clothes (male_casualsuit03, male_casualsuit04, female_casualsuit01, female_casualsuit02, shoes01, shoes02). ## Everything else

- Terrain, ocean (geometry nodes), hotel, cut-away, sky: built procedurally in `scripts/01_build.py`.
- DA KRAS FILMS logo: the client's own (`blender/assets/brand/`).
- Voiceover: the client's recording (Tsunami2004 v012 VO).
