## Made for this film (procedural, in `scripts/`)
- The parlour: damask wallpaper, wainscot, plank floor, the Persian-style rug, the sash window and velvet curtains, the garden outside (its season and daylight are keyed values).
- The camel-back settee and the footstool.
- The vibration ripples, the pulse dots and every light cue.

MPFB2 (GPL) is used only to build the characters. It is not needed to open or render the file.
