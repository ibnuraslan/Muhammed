## Assessment
The approved four rounds are used up. The remaining faults are structural, so another camera pass will not fix them:
- **Key grips:** the procedural IK pinch cannot keep the hand on a small key under the wrist limits. Each lock beat needs a hand-keyed grip.

Each fix is a re-stage, and I'd plan it before building anything.
