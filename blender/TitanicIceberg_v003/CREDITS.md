## Made for this film
- The iceberg is a procedural sculpt (Voronoi fracture, facet, crystal and striae displacement, ice SSS, and an animated red hull-paint mask).
- Also procedural: the ocean (Ocean modifier), fog, mist and funnel smoke volumes, sparks, the rooms, the explainer cyc and the photo print.
- The photo print is a render of the beat-1 view, toned as a 1912 sepia print.

No logos, text, ship names or likenesses of real people appear anywhere in the film.
