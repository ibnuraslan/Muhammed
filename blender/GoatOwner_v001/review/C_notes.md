## Changes since B
- **Camera:**
  - Goat-relative framings now use a heading frame. B had several looking up at the sky because of this.
  - Fixed the passes through the owner's body at 2.4 s and through a crate at 11 s.
  - Beat 2 now keeps the goat in frame while it snorts at the passer-by.
  - The post-impact section is now a real orbit around both characters, where B held one framing for 2 s.
  - The crane at 24–27 s now orbits as it rises.
  - The pasture ending is now filmed from the front, so the goat's face is at her palm.
  - The porch crane no longer sits behind the posts.
- **Impact:**
  - The goat now rears its head up into his midsection, and he leans in mid-swing.
  - The goat recoils, paws and shakes its head between the two hits.
  - The second hit no longer leaves the goat inside his legs.
- **Faces:** the jaw, upper and lower lids, brows and mouth corners are keyed through the MPFB face bones. He scowls and shouts at the goat, goes wide-eyed in shock on the charge and grimaces while staggering. His eyes are closed when he lies still, and the rescuer smiles.
- **Death beat:** he now falls forward and lies face down. The arms rest flat, the body sits on the ground with no sinking, and dust settles around him.
- **Time-lapse (beat 3):** the owner makes fast-motion menacing visits. Sun, sky tint and season light sweep across, and the goat visibly thins. The thinning is an inward displace, not a bone scale.
- **No text or logos:**
  - **Truck:** painted out a joke logo on the doors, cartoon eyes and the plate word. The plate word was embossed in the normal map, which is now flattened too. The printed-letter cargo boxes are now plain boards.
  - **Clothing:** removed the MakeHuman chest emblem from the rescuer's and official's suit.
  - **Sky:** removed the HDRI horizon band that showed a distant city.
- **Life:**
  - Trees sway with noise.
  - Wind-blown dust drifts through every farm beat.
  - The passer-by works the far field edge through beats 4–9.
  - The police tape flutters.
  - Pasture goats graze.
- **Other fixes:** the catch pole is now a thin pole held in both hands, where it was a log. The porch stair stringers have the correct slope. The officials stop at the foot of the ramp.
