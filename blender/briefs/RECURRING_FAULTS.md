# Recurring faults — fix BEFORE building, check every shot against this list

These appeared in nearly every batch-1 film. Treat each as a build rule, not a review note.

1. Limbs/bodies through things: legs through chairs/footrests, hands through tables/glass/faces, arms through torso, bodies sinking into beds/floors. Rule: every contact is placed on the surface (ray cast), clearance checked per frame.
2. Floating/hovering props: bricks above a pile, tools in mid-air, staples/stitches standing off the skin like bridges, characters hovering over chairs. Rule: everything rests on a surface or is held by a hand with real grip.
3. Props jumping: objects flying from hand to target, popping in/out, changing shape/size/colour between frames or shots. Rule: one model, parented to the hand until placed.
4. Twisting/broken limbs: rope-thin twisted forearms, wrists spinning like a helicopter, stretched rubber arms, knees with no bend. Rule: rotation limits on wrists/forearms; check deformation at mid-motion.
5. Stiff/robotic motion: arms hanging dead straight, T/A-pose, walks with locked arms, static holds over 1 s. Rule: bent elbows, arm swing, weight shift, an action in every shot.
6. Mannequin people: bald or helmet/disc-cap hair, faceless surgeons, doll faces, dead/pale/glowing eyes. Rule: real hair, visible eyes with catchlights and blinks.
7. Wardrobe: one-colour skin-tight onesies, skin-coloured clothes, nudity reads (shirtless, shower). Rule: separate garments in contrasting colours with folds; no nudity reads.
8. Framing: crotch-centred, headless, heads cut by edges, lens blockers (lamps, drapes, legs), empty/blurred/pink/white frames, camera through skin or walls. Rule: every frame shows the subject's face and action.
9. Story: the key visual of the story missing or contradicted (ear growing on the arm, lego inside the nose, stitched monkey). Rule: the core beat is shown clearly and stays consistent across shots.
10. Continuity: clothes, masks, gloves, colours, set and props must stay the same between shots unless the story changes them.
