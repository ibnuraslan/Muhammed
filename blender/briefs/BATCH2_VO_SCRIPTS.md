# Batch 2 — VO scripts (B01–B37)

Transcribed from the reference films for recording. Read each one as written; small word changes are fine. Each film is built straight to your recording, so record one mp3 per film and name it with its number (e.g. `B01.mp3`).

## B01 — Dog Shoots Owner
*~82 words*

The dog stepped on a loaded shotgun in the backseat of a truck and accidentally pulled the trigger. The bullet tore through the front seat and into a man's back, breaking several ribs and tearing a hole in his lung. He managed to call 911 and was taken to the hospital where he eventually recovered. The dog was taken to animal control but released back to his owner. But unfortunately, this dog was killed a few weeks later in an unrelated incident.

## B02 — Swallowed By A Whale
*~91 words*

A humpback whale almost swallowed a man by accident. At first he thought he was inside a great white shark, but he didn't feel any teeth and quickly realized he was actually inside of a whale. The man claimed to have struggled for around 30 seconds and says this made the whale surface and spit him out. He was quickly rescued and only suffered severe bruising and a dislocated knee. However, experts find this story hard to believe, since whales are incredibly harmless and wouldn't typically mistake a man for fish.

## B07 — Escaping Prison With Spoons

## B14 — Mom Lifts Car Off Son
