## Story beats (in order)
A man chisels a tunnel through a mountain alone → villagers laugh, but he comes back every day with a hammer and chisel → years earlier his wife fell from a cliff; the hospital was blocked by the mountain and the road went 40+ miles round → she died before they reached it → he swore no one else would suffer that → 22 years of chiselling until a path cuts through → as long as a football field; a quick route to the hospital that has saved countless lives

## Shots to stage simply (known risks)
Real person: generic, respectful; chisel in one hand, hammer in the other, each strike landing on the chisel head (no hand through rock); the wife's fall off-screen or as a cut to her lying injured (no face pressed into the ground); route map as 3D terrain with no "40 Miles" or "HOSPITAL" text; ageing shown through hair and beard
