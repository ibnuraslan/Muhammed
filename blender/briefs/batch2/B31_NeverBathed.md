# B31 NeverBathed: The Man Who Refused To Bathe For 60 Years (Amou Haji)

- **Reference:** `blender/briefs/batch2/B31_NeverBathed_reference_1fps.jpg`. It shows one frame per second of the reference film (32 s). Study its shot order and pacing. Do not copy its narration or its exact shots.

## Story beats (in order)
He believed bathing would make him sick, so he didn't wash for 60+ years → said dirt kept him healthy → left home, slept in a shallow pit, ate roadkill, smoked animal dung in a pipe, burned his hair instead of cutting it → became the world's dirtiest man → villagers finally convinced him to bathe, and he died shortly after

## Shots to stage simply (known risks)
Rebuild in a real desert village; the pipe in his fingers at the lips (not into the mouth); the burning hair as a small singe with no flames on the face; no flies swarm in front of the lens; the bath scene with hands on his shoulders (contact, not through); his death off-screen
