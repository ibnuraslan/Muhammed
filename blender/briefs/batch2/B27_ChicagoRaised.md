## Story beats (in order)
Rows of workers hand-crank jackscrews under a building, all at once → the building rises with people still inside → wooden cribbing is stacked under it to the new street level → new foundations are built while it hangs → the city was built on a swamp, so streets flooded and disease spread → they raised the city out of the swamp; Chicago still stands on those foundations

## Shots to stage simply (known risks)
Many workers turning cranks: the hands on the crank handles (grip), elbows bent, no arms through the cribbing; cutaway of the cribbing stack; 1850s street set; the swamp flood shown as dirty water, not a green glow; the modern skyline ending without logos
