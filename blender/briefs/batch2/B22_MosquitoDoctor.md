# B22 MosquitoDoctor: He Let A Mosquito Kill Him (yellow fever)

- **Reference:** `blender/briefs/batch2/B22_MosquitoDoctor_reference_1fps.jpg`. It shows one frame per second of the reference film (40 s). Study its shot order and pacing. Do not copy its narration or its exact shots.

## Story beats (in order)
A mosquito bites a sick boy → a doctor traps it in a glass vial and lets it bite his own hand to prove mosquitoes spread the disease → within a week: fever, yellow eyes, black vomit → organs fail, he dies within weeks → his death proved mosquitoes were the carriers, saving millions

## Shots to stage simply (known risks)
Vial in the fingers, mosquito on the back of the hand (not on the face or eye); keep the black vomit very mild or show it as a stain only; organ cutaway as clean anatomy; death off-screen
