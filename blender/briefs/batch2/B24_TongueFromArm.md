- **Reference:** `blender/briefs/batch2/B24_TongueFromArm_reference_1fps.jpg`. It shows one frame per second of the reference film (22 s). Study its shot order and pacing. Do not copy its narration or its exact shots.

## Story beats (in order)
90% of her cancerous tongue is removed → a muscle is cut from her arm → veins connected and nerves stitched inside her mouth → the muscle is shaped onto what's left of her tongue → doctors said she'd never speak; days later she says hello to her daughter

## Shots to stage simply (known risks)
Surgery as a clean anatomy cutaway on a colour-coded model, not a real open mouth with gore; instruments in gloved fingers; the ending with mother and daughter's faces readable
