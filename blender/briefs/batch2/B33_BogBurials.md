# B33 BogBurials: Ancient Florida Bog Reveals 168 Bodies (Windover)

- **Reference:** `blender/briefs/batch2/B33_BogBurials_reference_1fps.jpg`. It shows one frame per second of the reference film (54 s). Study its shot order and pacing. Do not copy its narration or its exact shots.
- **Style:** current. If OLD: take the story only, and build every scene with real sets in our current look.

## Story beats (in order)
8,000 years ago people laid their dead curled in a bog → pinned them down with wooden stakes so they wouldn't float up or be eaten → peat sealed them like a lid, no air or light, no decay → a construction crew hits a skull → archaeologists uncover 168 burials → stomachs still hold last meals, skulls still hold brain, bones wrapped in burial cloth, bodies still curled

## Shots to stage simply (known risks)
**Burials:** respectful; people placing the bodies (hands under the shoulders, contact not through); skeletons clean, no gore. **Underwater:** must stay readable, no murky frames. **Text:** no "8000 YEARS" text. **Stakes:** they surround the body; none pass through it
