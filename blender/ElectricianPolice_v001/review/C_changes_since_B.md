# ElectricianPolice: changes since checkpoint B

- **Rider:** his hands and feet were mirrored onto the wrong side of the bike. They're fixed: both hands now grip the bars and his left foot goes down on his left as he stops. Hair and shirt now ripple in the wind while he rides and settle as he stops.
- **Officer:** he now faces the rider. He waves him down, writes the ticket on the pad in his left hand and passes the slip hand to hand to the rider's right hand. The rider turns toward him and shakes his head, annoyed.
- **Feet:** they now plant on each set's real floor height: road, shoulder, gravel yard and office/station floors. Each character's shoe-sole offset is measured from the mesh. The audit went from 531 sink frames to 0.
- **Substation gate:**
  - The double gate is now one gate.
  - It swings open on its hinge as he unlocks it and pushes it.
  - He walks through the opening rather than through the fence.
- **Hard hats:** they're re-modelled with a taller shell and no spike ridge, and seated on the measured top of the hair. He takes his hat off at the desk; it sits on the desk so his face reads during the grin.
- **Grin and smirk:** these now use the lip-corner face bones (oris03 with risorius). Beat 6 has a slow grin, and the end has a one-sided smirk.
- **Hi-vis vest:** it's rebuilt from shirt faces with a relaxed cut edge, which gives far fewer jaggies. His shirt is red in every shot.
- **Camera:**
  - The flight from the office to the station no longer clips the office wall, the pylon or the 4-storey blocks. It now backs out of the window, rises over the roof and then crosses the road high.
  - At the laptop it now frames his face from the screen side instead of sitting inside his hat.
- **Traffic:** the rickshaw that blocked the camera at 0.5–1 s and 3 s now runs along the kerb and stops short of the officer.
- **Laptop UI:** the record bars load in one after another as he scrolls (8.7–10.2 s). Grid pulses on the lines are bigger.
- **Station at dusk:** it's dark blue and moody rather than black, and there's a phone-torch spot. A visible beam cone was tried and dropped because it read as a grey disc. Camera rays see a clean dusk sky.
- **Clock:** the maker's mark under the 12 is covered.
- **Final shot:** he strolls up the road edge past the dark station, stops and turns to camera, with no door or step collisions.
