## Changes made to the CC-BY assets
- **Monkey:** split at the neck and capped. It is used for both monkeys, both seated upright, and has a UV gaze shift, eyelid shells and ear shape keys.
- **Brain:** rescaled into the macaque's skull, with an emissive x-ray material.
- **Oscilloscope:** its printed textures are flattened to plain colours so no text shows. Our own ECG trace runs on the screen.
- **Hospital Bed:** used as the operating table.
