## Built for this film (ours)
The donation bin and its one-way flap mechanism (hinge pin, counterweight, stop bar, pawl, spring), the base clothes heap under the scanned ones, the turnout colouring, gloves and sleeves on the firefighters' MPFB bodies, the woman's plain tee (the MPFB casualsuit01 texture with its logo and piping painted out), the blanket, sparks, dust motes, the x-ray shader and the studio-gradient world.

No logos, brand names or real-person likenesses are used. All characters are generic MPFB humans.
