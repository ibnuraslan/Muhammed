Some changes were made to these assets:
- The piano's maker lettering and fused keys were removed, and 88 hinged keys were built in their place.
- The skull was refitted, with its jaw opened.
- The ear parts were mirrored, scaled and recoloured.

## CC0 assets (MakeHuman community, built with MPFB2)
- the base body, rig and skin
- `toigo_male_suit_3` (suit), `toigo_ankle_boots_male` and `cortu_short_messy_hair`
- the eyes, eyebrows, teeth and tongue

The composer is a generic MakeHuman face. It is not a likeness of any real portrait.

## Made for this film
These were all built procedurally:
- the room and its panelling
- the rod, its clamp and the rings
- the music desk and pages (generated staves, with no text)
- the candles
- the x-ray shaders and the pulses
