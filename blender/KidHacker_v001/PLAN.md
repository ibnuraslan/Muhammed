# B32 KidHacker: plan (Phase 1, before the VO)

Vertical 1080×1920, 60 fps, Cycles. Shot order follows the reference sheet (62 s, 1 fps). Every shot is its own
clip, about 30% longer than its reference beat, so it can be trimmed to the VO in Phase 2 without stretching.
Provisional P1 timeline: frames 1–5052 (33 shots, hard cuts, one marker camera per shot).

**Look:** night library in readable blue (CRT glow + cool fill on her face, warm shelf lamps), bright grey-blue
FBI office, cool grey interrogation room. **No text anywhere.** Screens are flat emissive shapes only (windows,
bars, dots, icons, a generic gold seal with a star, a red padlock). No FBI or NASA logos.

**Logo (DA KRAS FILMS):**
1. Dark logo on the beige CRT bezel below the screen (like a maker's badge). Flat to camera, about 30% of the
   frame width, readable in S05–S07, S10, S14–S15, S17, S19 (more than 1 s each).
2. Dark logo on the cardboard archive box on the FBI desk (S20, small; the CRT one is the main placement).

**Cast:** Kid (10-year-old girl, long brown hair parted in the middle, floral blouse, jeans), Mom (blue blouse,
auburn hair), Agent B and Agent C (dark suits). All MPFB characters from earlier studio packages, refitted;
eye fix + a camera catchlight on every shot; teeth hidden (mouths closed or slightly parted).
