## Made for this film (procedural, in `scripts/`)
- The house: facade, tiled roof, windows, shutters, flower bed, lawn, fence, the casement window with its crack and 36 glass shards.
- The gaming room: walls, LED strips, desk, monitor with a text-free game scene, keyboard, mouse, speakers, tower PC, gaming chair, shelf, abstract canvas.
- The headset: a generic over-ear headset with no logo or brand, fitted to his head (band, pads, yokes, cups, cushions), with its crack decal, crater and chips.
- The bullet: a generic 9 mm round-nose slug. There's no cartridge and no firearm.
- The ear-cup cross-section: shell, padding foam, metal frame, cushion, the bullet track and foam squeeze (geometry nodes), sparks and the gradient backdrop.
- The wall hole, the plaster chips and the impact flashes.

MPFB2 (GPL) is used only to build the character. You don't need it to open or render the file.
