## Independent reviewer verdict: FAIL (I agree after checking the frames)
1. **Blocker, f886–961:** reaching for the band, his right hand passes over his forehead (f891) and onto the top of his hair (f896). The forearm then goes up beside and behind his head.
2. **Blocker, f1011–1026 and f1101–1166:** while he holds the headset, his arm is straight out to the side like a mannequin's. The headset is at, or past, the left frame edge. On "realized it had just saved his life" the cracked cup isn't in frame for about 1 s.
3. **Minor, f751–766:** a thin trail still shows at the cup after the bullet leaves.
4. **Minor:** the T-shirt clings at the chest in front views.
5. **Minor, f36–46:** window bars are large as the camera passes through.

## Suggested fix (needs director approval; my fix budget is used)
- Stage the removal from the front: both hands on the outer faces of the cups, with elbows forward and out. Lift up and forward.
- Hold the headset in both hands at chest height, with the elbows bent and the cracked cup to camera at about 1/3 of the frame.
