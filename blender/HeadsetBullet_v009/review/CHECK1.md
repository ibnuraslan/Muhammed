# HeadsetBullet v009: review of the final preview (fix rounds used: 2 of 2)

Preview: `review/preview.mp4`, rendered from the packed `HeadsetBullet_v009_60fps.blend` (every 5th frame, 270x480, with the VO). Contact pages: `review/pages/p01-p05.jpg`.

## Independent reviewer verdict: FAIL
The reviewer was a fresh subagent with no access to my notes.

**Blockers:**
1. **961–1006:** the headset hangs above his head while his hands stay open. His fingers don't close on the cups or the band.
2. **1011–1021:** his forearm crosses his face as the headset comes down.
3. **1026–1101 and 1176–1201:** the held headset reads as floating beside an open, palm-up hand. It isn't gripped.
4. **151–231:** the slow-motion bullet barely moves beside him for about 1.3 s. It reads like the old "floating prop" fault.
5. **1–100:** the bullet is only a faint streak outside, hard to see.
6. **596–609 and 746–758:** the warm impact flashes, about 0.2 s each, read as empty orange frames.
7. **724–744:** the cross-section stays empty after the bullet leaves.
8. **1121–1146:** his face is half out of frame when he turns to the wall.

**Minor:**
- Near-static holds at 351–496, 611–706 and 761–846.
- At 1151–1201 he never clearly looks at the cracked cup.
- In the 531–591 macro the cup looks large.
- At 891–921 his hands enter from the frame edges.

**Passes:**
- The logo is readable (331–600 and 1111–1176).
- The bullet strike, crack, chips and sparks read well (516–591).
- The headset sits snugly while he games.
- The room stays consistent.
- The eyes are fine, and so are the clothes.
