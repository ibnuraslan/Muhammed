## Made for this film (procedural, in `scripts/`)
- The living room, the French window (frames, glass, the crack), the shutters, the terrace, the facade, the wall art (abstract colour blocks, no text) and the brass pendant.
- The bullet: a generic 9 mm round-nose slug. There is no cartridge and no firearm.
- The cross-section: shirt, skin, fat lobules, three fascia sheets, muscle fibres, peritoneum, bowel loops, the wound track and squeeze (geometry nodes), and the gradient backdrop.
- The shirt hole, the impact flash and the air trail.

MPFB2 (GPL) is used only to build the character. It is not needed to open or render the file.
