# PopeShot: plan (checkpoint A)

27.0 s, 1080×1920, 60 fps, frames 1–1620. One camera (`CAM`) on one smooth spline of keyed framings (one framing every ~1 s), with no cuts and no camera switching. Cycles CPU, AgX Medium High Contrast.
Every set lives in one scene, and the sets are spaced out along the camera path (square → x-ray studio → theatre → balcony → cell → shrine → x-ray → shrine), so each transition is a physical move: a dive through cloth, a pass through a wall, a whip-pan or a rack focus.
The Pope is a **generic** elderly MPFB man in plain white cassock, cape and zucchetto (skullcap). There is no likeness and no insignia or text anywhere. There is no blood and no wound detail; the bullet path is shown in x-ray only.

## Trickiest shots and how I'll do them
1. **The crowd at scale:** 40 MPFB humans is heavy. Build about 8 base humans, then make linked-data duplicates with offset action copies (different start frames) for crowd life. Decimate the far rows.
2. **The bullet ride into the x-ray (beat 2):** the camera is parented to nothing; the bullet and camera keys share a slow-motion section. The cloth dive uses a macro-weave texture (≤6 frames). The x-ray is the goat-film porthole technique: robe and skin go transparent by distance from an empty, and the skeleton and organs sit inside, attached to MPFB bones.
3. **The collapse into the aides (beat 3):** MPFB IK: aides' hands are IK targets under the Pope's armpits, the Pope's root is keyed down, and he plants his feet. Grasp checked numerically (hand-to-body BVH distance).
4. **Transitions without cuts:** a pigeon-wing wipe (beat 3→4), a bloom through the window (4→5), a fly through the wall (5→6), candlelight beams (6→7), a glint dissolve (7→8). The camera stays one continuous spline throughout.
5. **Size limit:** 95 MB with no LFS. 1K textures (2K only for the hero robe, marble and x-ray bones), a 2K HDRI, decimated crowd, `orphans_purge`, and saving compressed.
