## Made for this film (procedural, in `BellyFat_v001/scripts/`)
- Supermarket: floor, shelving with rails and blank tags, unbranded products (bevelled cartons, rimmed cans, shouldered bottles; colour-block labels with no text or logos), coolers, signs, lights, cart, basket, carton.
- Bullet: a generic slug only. No cartridge and no firearm.
- Cross-section: fabric with weave, skin layers, about 7.5k fat lobules (geometry nodes), capillaries, fascia, muscle fibres, peritoneum, mesentery, energy rings, studio backdrop.

MPFB2 (GPL) only builds the characters. It is not needed to open or render the file.
