## Modifications to the CC-BY assets
- **Monkey.** The mesh is split at the neck and capped, then used for both monkeys. One copy's fur is hue-shifted ginger. I added an iris UV shift for gaze, eyelid shells, and ear shape keys.
- **Brain.** It is rescaled and given an emissive x-ray material.
- **Oscilloscope.** The printed model name is covered with a plate, and my own ECG trace is shown on its screen.
