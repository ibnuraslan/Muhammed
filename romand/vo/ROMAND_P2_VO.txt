In 1975, Romand has not passed the examination that allows him to advance beyond his second year. But he tells the people around him that he has succeeded. It is a lie he can still correct. He can admit the failure, repeat the year openly, or leave medicine. Instead, he keeps the successful version of himself in circulation.

At university, he repeatedly registers for the same year while maintaining the appearance of progressing through his studies. He attends courses and reads medical material. There are books and notes for other people to see. As the years pass, his friends are completing real stages of their training, while he describes stages of a career he is inventing.

One of those friends later remembers seeing him surrounded by the equipment of a serious student: folders, journals, lecture notes. He has no reason to treat a friend’s account of passing an examination as a claim that needs investigating. Romand has attended medical school with him. He knows the subject. The story fits what is already visible.

Romand is acquiring medical knowledge. What he lacks is the qualification and professional life he claims it represents.

In 1980, he marries Florence Crolet. They have known each other through their families since they were young. She trains as a pharmacist. Their circle includes medical professionals, people who might seem well placed to recognize an impostor. Yet Romand can discuss medical research convincingly enough to belong among them.

Over time, he gives himself a position at the World Health Organization in Geneva. He presents himself as a researcher, which also provides an explanation for why he does not have an ordinary medical practice. His work takes place elsewhere, inside a large international institution.

The family eventually lives in Prévessin-Moëns, on the French side of the Swiss border. Geneva is close enough for the journey to work to be entirely plausible. Romand leaves home for a working day. But there is no office expecting him, no research team waiting for his contribution, and no salary arriving from the organization.
