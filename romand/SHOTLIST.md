# Romand film: shot list v1 (for hub review)

8 parts, **236 shots**, about **18:56.7** at 140 wpm (estimated; retime to the recorded VO).
Generated by `romand/shots/build_shotlist.py` from `shots_data.py`. Each shot's VO is sliced out of `romand/vo/ROMAND_Pn_VO.txt` and the slices are checked to rebuild every file exactly: **no word of the narration is changed**.

## Rules applied

- Look: STYLE_V2 (diorama in a black void, amber-gold world, faceless pale-clay mannequins in clothes, cool key + warm rim, one RED accent). Explainers (EXP) use the Engagement-rules slab: dark blue-grey, key object #E3FF00.
- Pacing: cold open (P1-01 to P1-23) cuts every 1.5–4 s, hook ends on a hard stop at ~10 s (P1-04). Body shots average about 5–6 s (a little faster than the reference films' 7–8.5 s median), all with camera or subject motion.
- Kinds alternate (CIN cinematic / EXP explainer / MAC macro / MAP / 2D card); no three of the same in a row where avoidable.
- RED accent items only: nylon cord, tear-gas can, WHO badge (the one that never existed), rolling pin and rifle (silhouettes, never in use), petrol can, ankle tag.
- Sensitivity: no violence on screen. The attack in the forest is seen from far above, withdrawing. The killings are implied by empty rooms, closed doors, the staircase and the house fire from outside. The children appear only from behind watching a cartoon and playing in the garden. No gunshot SFX: silence drops instead. Nothing beyond the script is shown as fact (Emmanuel's interpretation stays abstract).
- Disclaimer: `disclaimer(discretion=True)` straight after the cold open and title (P1-24/25).
- Person cards use our own faceless mannequin renders, never real photos (victims especially). Children get no portrait (a coat on a hook).
- 0 clipping: every animated shot gets the per-frame clip check (bags, briefcases, doors, seats) before the final render.
