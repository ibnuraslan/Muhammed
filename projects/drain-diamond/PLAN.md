# The $25 Million Hidden in a Drain — shot plan

2.35:1, 1920×816, 24 fps, house grade. Paris palette: limestone, slate roofs, cool grey rain; warm boutique light is the one accent.
Every shot: slow camera move + one thing moving in frame. No maps, no graphics, no logos, no hand close-ups.
People are unnamed MakeHuman figures. Guns are shapes at medium distance, never at camera.

Recurring sets: **DRAIN** (yard of the suburban house), **BOUTIQUE** (nameless Avenue Montaigne jeweller, inside and out),
**STAIRS** (service stairs), **UNIT** (robbery unit office), **CAR** (surveillance), **RAID** (dawn raid / stair compartment),
**GYM**, **COURT** (Paris tribunal), **TABLE** (evidence table).
