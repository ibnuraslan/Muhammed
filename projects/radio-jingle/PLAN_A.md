# The Kidnapper Who Only Played a Radio Jingle: plan, part A (Cold Open, Chapters One and Two)

Every scene is cinematic: low late sun or overcast, one hard key light, dark fill, haze in the woods, teal-and-amber grade.
Ursula appears only at the piano (from behind) and on the red bicycle (from behind, at a distance). She is never in the box and there is no body.
Real people are unnamed figures. The only on-screen text is the cut-out letter.
Reused sets: HOUSE (piano room, hallway phone, kitchen), PATH (lakeside path and bend), FOREST (grid search, clearing), BOX (forensic reconstruction), VILLAGE (street, Fiat).
