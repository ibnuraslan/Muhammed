# Radio Jingle, part B (Ch3, Ch4, Ch5): callout cards (proposal for the hub)

Shared format: `nvidia_doc/scenes/callout.py` (A's branch @ff086eb, copied from killer-in-house @383c57c). Person cards go lower RIGHT and place cards lower LEFT. Each card is a black-and-white free-licence photo with a white border, the name in typewriter type and a role line below it. Cards slide in, hold about 3 s and slide out. They never fade, and appear at the first naming only.
Times are the B storyboard estimates (184 wpm, 3 s title lead, long takes at their own length; see `review/B_STORYBOARD_FRAMES/INDEX.md`). They are part-relative (chapter start = 00:00) and get re-timed to the word once the VO lands.
A card never goes up while a chapter title is on screen (3 s lead). Not repeated from part A: Eching, Schondorf, Ursula, Michael.
