# STORYBOARD v2 — Chapters 3–5 (chat B)

Rework after studying the 8 reference breakdowns (`claude/fern-analysis: research/refs/`). Inspiration only: no shot or design is copied.

**Rules for v2:**
- Every literal or filler shot is replaced by a physical idea: facts written into the set, counts as objects, measure lines, objects on black.
- Evidence recurs like a cast: the tape print, the wire, the Grundig, the struck-through statement, the debt letters.
- Each chapter gets one long hero take at its peak, with short inserts around it.
- People stay faceless or from behind. Ursula is never shown. The convicted man never acts.

**Hero long takes:**
- Ch3: the wire from above (≈ 26 s).
- Ch4: the reconstruction chain on one table (≈ 45 s).
- Ch5: the receiver off the hook (≈ 25 s, ring ending on the hallway set).

**Recurring devices shared with chat A:**
- One map system: charcoal paper, brick-red line, cream labels.
- The box, the rotary phone, the police reel deck and the kitchen radio models.
- "Court reconstruction" label style.
