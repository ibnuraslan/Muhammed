# They Paid $30 for Radioactive Scrap — plan

Recurring sets (one location each, reused across chapters):
**Street** (İkitelli street + the open lot across from the relative's house, winter 1998, overcast) ·
**Yard** (the family yard beside the house, open to the street) · **Building** (the Istanbul building / warehouse the
packages sat in) · **Clinic** (local clinic) · **Hospital** (large hospital: ward, bedside, corridor, nurses' station) ·
**Scrapyard** (hero: the second scrapyard) · **Smelter** (smelting works east of Istanbul) · **Therapy room**
(hospital radiotherapy room with a cobalt machine) · **Storeroom** (Ankara storeroom) · **Records** (investigators'
office). Nothing is a map or graphic; the camera always drifts slowly and something in frame always moves.

**Master timing (person's rule, Nov 2026):** the intro has 2.5 s of picture before the first word. Each chapter has at
least **3.0 s** of picture under its title card before the voice starts (make_master lead = 3.0 for chapters).
Titles: new title_card style (long titles on 2 lines, soft scrim, one PNG per title).
**Delivery (Banker lesson):** unpack textures into one shared deduped folder (<=2K), share identical meshes, measure
peak RAM opening the master, zip into <=380 MB parts with one shared top folder plus a MERGE .bat.
**Delivery:** ONE film file (intro + Seldom sting + chapters) with one audio pair (with and without voice), PNG output.
