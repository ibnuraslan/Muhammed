# Radioactive Scrap — STORYBOARD v2

Before/after sheet: `review/storyboard_v2_before_after.jpg` (low-res test stills).
Motion previews (~480p, 12 fps, low samples): `review/previews/*.mp4`.
All new shots are real 3D objects in lit Cycles scenes (look v5): no flat, vector or illustrated graphics. The reference
films were used for ideas only. No real logos or names, and no invented numbers: every figure on a prop is from the script.

## Recurring hero objects
The red route map (Ch1 3B → Ch1 13B → Ch2 12A → Ch4 5A), the container (whole, cut open, as two shells), the capsule.

## Animation pass
- Every person: frame-driven "alive" layer in `place()`: breathing, slow weight shift, unhurried head turns (each person
  has their own phase; upper body only, so feet stay planted). On top of each shot's action.
- Hands: fingers curl about their own knuckles (fixed claws everywhere); hand QA crops as before.
- Objects: the pen draws, pins drop, the stamp comes down, plates lift, the needle climbs, chalk draws, magnets go up,
  strings draw, checks appear, the bulb and pen sway; existing shots keep sparks, dust, drums, doors, leaves, lights.
- Camera: every shot moves (slow push, track, or tilt with a moving focus target).
- Shot length: short inserts around one long hero take per chapter, set when the VO timings arrive:
  Ch1 5B (the man reaches into the opening), Ch2 5B (the trefoil comes into the light), Ch3 17A/B (the fourteenth
  try), Ch4 11A (he walks out into the winter light). Lists in the VO (dates, counts) cut on the word.
