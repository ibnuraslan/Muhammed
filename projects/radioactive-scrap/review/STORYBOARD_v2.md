# Radioactive Scrap - STORYBOARD v2.1 (user feedback applied)

Before/after sheet: `review/storyboard_v2_1_before_after.jpg`. Full storyboard: `review/STORYBOARD_FULL.pdf`.

Rules now in force: nothing moves by itself (every moving object is moved by a person, a machine or a physical cause);
drawn marks must look physically real; every human action means something. Realistic 3D, look v5, no flat graphics.
Highlights are done the way cinema does it (push-in, focus pull, light, a person's attention), or with a soft FILLED
warm ripple on the target (never thin line rings): `rs_kit.ripple()`.
