# Thumbnail concepts: Drain, Killer in the House, Radio Jingle

Concepts and text only. Nothing here is rendered yet; the thumbnail chat renders after approval.
Highlighted word = `[WORD]` (lime/yellow, or red box where noted). All text is white condensed (Anton/Bebas), top-left or top-centre; bottom-right always empty for the timestamp.

## Why the old ones felt boring

| Problem | Example | Fix used below |
|---|---|---|
| Text repeats the picture | Diamond on a brick + "THEY HID $25M IN A DRAIN" | Text gives the one fact the image can't show (a number, a contradiction, a time) |
| Hero object on a plinth, no story | Diamond on a pedestal | Show the object *where it shouldn't be*, or a cutaway that reveals a hidden layer |
| No question | "SEALED UNDER A DRAIN" | Each line leaves a "how?" or "who?" only the video answers |

## Patterns borrowed (not copied)

| Channel | Pattern | Where it's used here |
|---|---|---|
| Fern | Cross-section / cutaway of a real place, one hard light, 2–4 words | Drain D1, Killer K1 |
| MrBallen / Coffeehouse Crime | An ordinary place + one wrong detail; short "creepy fact" line | Drain D2, Killer K2, K4 |
| LEMMiNO | Single object on black, very few words, a question mark | Radio R2 |
| Magnates Media | Big number + contrast (cheap vs. valuable) | Drain D4 |
| Wendover | Top-down "map" view with one thing marked | Drain D2 |

## 1. The $25 Million Hidden in a Drain

Facts used (script): police searched the house 5 times; on the 5th they saw a slightly lighter patch of concrete in a rainwater drain, drilled it, found a plastic container with 19 rings incl. the 31-carat diamond (~€6M alone); whole cache ~$25M (AP). "Missed it 4 times" = the stone was found on the 5th search of the house (script); the render chat should keep the wording to "searches", not claim each search checked the drain. Wiretap: Yahiaoui mentioned "a little cement work". Robbers of 2007 spent the night inside the shop (guard let them in).

| # | Pitch | Image (camera · hero · light · palette) | Text | Curiosity gap | Why it beats generic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Cutaway of the drain** | Eye-level cross-section through the courtyard ground: top third wet grey concrete + drain grate + one slightly lighter square; below the cut, dark soil, a plain white plastic tub, lid ajar, one ring catching a single hard spotlight. Night-blue ambient, warm key on the ring only | `SEARCH [#5]` (small line above: "POLICE MISSED IT 4 TIMES") | How did they miss it four times, and what tipped them off on the fifth? | Shows the *hiding*, not just the loot; the number is a fact you can't see |
| D2 | **Spot the $25M** | Straight top-down of the courtyard (stone house wall, drainpipe, grate, potted plant). One concrete patch a shade lighter, ringed with a red police-chalk circle. Overcast, cool grey; the circle is the only colour | `$25M IS IN THIS [PHOTO]` | Where? Viewer scans the picture, then clicks | Turns the thumbnail into a game; the "wrong detail" pattern |
| D3 | **"A little cement work"** | Low macro on the drill bit just breaking through the lighter patch, the hole is already drilled (no flying chips), drill resting beside it, a torch beam shows a diamond glint inside the hole. Torch-white beam, rest near black | `"A LITTLE [CEMENT] WORK"` | Who said that, and why did it crack the case? | A real quote from the wiretap; reads as a clue, not a caption |
| D4 | **Cheap box, priceless ring** | Object-on-black: a scuffed, dirty white plastic food tub, lid off, the 31-ct ring sitting on grimy rings in it. One hard top light | `[€6M] RING. PLASTIC TUB.` | Why would anyone store a €6M stone in a food box? | Price-vs-junk contrast is instantly readable at 168 px |
| D5 | **They slept in the shop** | Dark luxury boutique at night: glowing display cases, and on the carpet behind a counter four pairs of paint-splattered work boots and rolled overalls. Cold moonlight + warm case lights | `THEY [SLEPT] INSIDE` | Who let them in the night before? (the guard) | Pivots to the heist's real twist; very different from every "diamond" thumbnail |
| D6 | **Fifth time's the charm** | Courtyard at dusk, three faceless officers' legs/torches in the background out of focus; foreground: a small yellow evidence marker "5" next to the drain patch | `THEY CAME BACK [5] TIMES` | What made them so sure it was here? | Uses persistence as the hook; marker is a real-world prop, not a graphic |

## 2. The Killer Was Still in the House

Facts used: Denver 1941; Philip Peters killed Oct 17 1941; Theodore Coneys lived in the attic crawl space reached through a closet-ceiling opening ~8 × 15 inches; roof sloped both sides, no room to stand; hot plate + radio on a rigged outlet; police searched the house more than once; neighbours saw lights; pulled down July 30 1942, more than 9 months after the killing. No likeness of Coneys or Peters.

| # | Pitch | Image | Text | Curiosity gap | Why it beats generic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | **Dollhouse cutaway** | Side cross-section of the brick house at night, like an architect's section: lit rooms below with two faceless detectives with torches in the hall; above them, under the roof slope, a tiny dark crawl space with one warm hot-plate glow and a thin figure lying flat (silhouette only). Blue night, one warm spot in the attic | `[9 MONTHS] ABOVE THEM` | How do you live 9 months over the people looking for you? | Shows two layers at once: the search and the hiding place. Fern-style, very readable |
| K2 | **The hole in the closet** | Looking straight up inside a narrow closet: coats at the frame edge, a small dark rectangle in the ceiling, chalk marks "8 in / 15 in" beside it, a faint orange glow leaking from the opening. Bare-bulb top light | `HE LIVED IN [HERE]` + arrow to the hole | A grown man in a hole that small, for how long? | Extreme scale contrast; one simple shape for phones |
| K3 | **The empty house is lit** | Exterior, winter night, snow on the lawn, every window dark; only the small attic vent glows warm. Police car parked across the street | `THE HOUSE WAS [EMPTY]` | Then who turned on the light? | The "wrong detail" pattern; creepy without gore |
| K4 | **Legs into the ceiling** | Low angle up from the closet floor: a man's legs in torn trousers half-way through the ceiling opening, a detective's hand reaching for one ankle. Torch beam from below | `HE WAS [STILL] UP THERE` | Up where, since when? | The real moment from the case; action frozen, no faces |
| K5 | **Searched while he listened** | In the attic: foreground a hot plate and small radio by an outlet; through gaps in the floorboards, torch light from the room below. Warm/cold split | `POLICE SEARCHED. HE [LISTENED].` | How did they not find him? | Puts the viewer in the killer's spot; contradiction |
| K6 | **The haunted house** | Kitchen at night: icebox door open, half-eaten roast, a jar of preserves missing from a row on the shelf (gap in the row). Single bulb | `[NOT] A GHOST` | Who was taking the food? | Plays the "haunted" angle the script sets up; tiny wrong detail (the gap) |

## 3. Radio Jingle (the kidnapper who only played a jingle)

Facts used: the caller never spoke, only played the Bayern 3 traffic jingle; the mother asked for the names of two stuffed toys as proof of life; silence. Faint clicks on the police tape; a forensic expert said a seized Grundig TK 248 was *probably* one of the machines used; 11 comparable recorders tested; conviction 2010, still contested; the victim's brother Michael (music teacher, sound background) challenged the sound evidence. No child, no box with the child, no victim imagery.

| # | Pitch | Image | Text | Curiosity gap | Why it beats generic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **The silent caller** | Night phone booth, close: a small portable cassette player rested on the booth shelf, speaker pressed to the hanging handset's mouthpiece (handset rests on the shelf too — physically stable). Sodium-orange street light, booth glass reflections | `HE NEVER SAID A [WORD]` | How do you catch someone who never spoke? | The method is the hook; image explains "how" and text sharpens it |
| R2 | **One of eleven** | Line-up: eleven reel-to-reel recorders in a row on black, ten in shadow, one under a hard spotlight (the 4-10B shot). | `[1] OF 11` + small line "CAUGHT BY A CLICK?" | Why that one, and is the evidence right? | Police-lineup pattern on objects; instantly readable |
| R3 | **The click** | Macro on the recorder's VU meter needle kicking to red, reels blurred behind. Warm amber | `A [CLICK] CONVICTED HIM?` | Can a click on a 1981 tape prove anything? | Tiny detail, big stakes; the question mark matches the film's doubt |
| R4 | **Her brother doubts it** | A man from behind (faceless) in headphones at a desk with a waveform on screen, piano in the dark background | `HER BROTHER [DOUBTS] IT` | Why would the victim's own brother question the conviction? | Strongest emotional contradiction in the script |
| R5 | **Proof of life** | Old 1981 telephone on a hall table, handset off the hook, a notepad with two words crossed out ("names?") — no toys shown. Lamp light | `SHE ASKED. [SILENCE].` | What did she ask, and why no answer? | Quiet and human; no victim shown |
| R6 | **The jingle as weapon** | Object on black: a portable radio dial lit on the station frequency, nothing else | `THE KIDNAPPER'S [VOICE]` with the radio as the "voice" | A radio as a voice? | LEMMiNO-style minimalism; strong silhouette |

Note on R6 and any shot from inside the box: the radio found in the box is a real detail but sits next to the death of a child. Keep the box out of the thumbnail.

## Checklist for the render chat (all concepts)

| Rule | How it's met |
|---|---|
| No real likeness / logos / victims | Faceless, silhouettes or objects only; no shop name, no station logo, no child or box interior |
| Bottom-right clear | Text sits top-left or top-centre; hero object centre/left |
| True to script | Every number above is in the script (5th search, 4 missed, $25M, €6M, 8×15 in, 9 months, 11 recorders) |
| Nothing impossible | No flying chips, no floating ring; handset and player rest on the booth shelf |
