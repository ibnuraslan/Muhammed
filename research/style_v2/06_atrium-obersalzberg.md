## Characters and staging

| Item | Obs | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Protagonist | A courier **seated in the car** for most of the film (through the windshield, OTS from the back seat, hands on the wheel) | A vehicle is the perfect seated stage |
| Guards | Stand at barriers; checks shown as OTS through the car window; a frisk by the car | Short, contained actions |
| Hands | Inserts only: envelope, pistol, wave | Same rule as Yarnhub |
| Walk | One walk through the house, shown in feet-only inserts, back views and doorways | Walks are broken into pieces |
| Climax | Hitler's face at MCU, a scream (VO), fist raised in a wide | Emotion via face + VO, not body |

### Techniques (≤15-word examples)

| Technique | Example | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Sealed object | "a letter no one but Hitler is allowed to see" | Curiosity carried to the end |
| Levels | "he must pass three armed checkpoints" | The viewer tracks progress (hexagon numbers + bar) |
