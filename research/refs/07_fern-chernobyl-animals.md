## Techniques observed

| Area | What Fern does |
|---|---|
| Hook | Pure mood: fog, twisted trees, deformed animals, then the location reveal |
| Point of view | Animals as protagonists (deer, dog) instead of people |
| Long holds | 3D median 11.5 s, graphics median 19 s; 55–90 s single shots for whole explanations |
| Evidence | Real footage and photos shown inside rounded "cards" (carousel), clearly separate from 3D |
| Science | DNA, particle decay, "→ MELANIN →" one-word equations, family tree, Petri dishes |
| Honesty | "Abstract visualization" / "Simplified visualization" tags in the corner. The scientist's doubts are kept in |
| Palette | Green-grey murk for the zone, toxic yellow for radiation, orange for the war. Black backgrounds for science |
