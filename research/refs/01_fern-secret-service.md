| Time | Block | Animated role |

**Pattern:** the fictional present-day plot is the spine. Each history chapter explains *why* a protocol exists, then the story cuts back to the fiction to show that protocol working. The cliffhanger is the shot already fired at 0:55. It pays off at 18:05 and 23:05.

## Techniques observed

| Area | What Fern does |
|---|---|
| Hook | 60 s of pure 3D action with a cliffhanger (bullet frozen mid-impact), then 3 questions on faces |
| Spine | A fictional present-day scenario runs through all chapters. History explains it, the fiction shows it |
| VO → shot | Lists = one shot per item. Key paragraph = one long move (19–42 s). Archival = rapid 1–1.5 s cuts |
| Abstract facts | Red line on a real newspaper, dashed measurement in 3D space, top-down tactical maps, icons on charcoal paper, document with red highlight, timer readout |
| Camera | Top-down "god view" for space and logistics. Low dolly for vehicles. ECU on eyes/hands for emotion. Symmetric frontal shots for institutions |
| Light/colour | Dark scenes with one coloured rim (red or blue). Warm tungsten pools for period interiors. Red = danger graphics. Daylight scenes kept neutral |
| Characters | Stylised-realistic faces (game-cinematic level), no exact likeness of real people. **Hands and eyes in ECU** do the emotional work. Crowds are dense instanced figures behind DOF |
| Labels | Quote-style uppercase role labels ("COUNTER ASSAULT"). Place/date tag lower-left. Source credit tiny bottom-left on archival |
| Chapters | "CHAPTER II/III" huge condensed uppercase + one-line subtitle, set over a 3D scene from the chapter |

## What Seldom can take

| # | Technique | How in our pipeline | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fictional present-day spine intercut with history chapters | Script structure: write a "scenario" thread and cut back to it after each chapter | Easy (script) |
| 2 | Cold-open cliffhanger frozen mid-action, paid off later | Same scene file, render the freeze frame (motion blur off, DOF on) and the continuation later | Easy |
| 3 | One noun = one shot for lists | `chapter_shots.py`: list beats → N short shots of 1.5–3 s each | Easy |
| 4 | Long tracking shot carrying a whole paragraph | Camera on a path constraint, 15–20 s, slow. Fewer renders per minute | Easy |
| 5 | Red route/line drawn on a real document | Document plane with scanned texture + Grease Pencil or curve bevel with an animated `bevel_factor_end` | Easy |
| 6 | Measurement overlay in 3D space ("4.5 m") | Dashed curve between two empties + text object, top-down ortho camera | Easy |
| 7 | Top-down tactical map of a venue | Ortho camera over a simplified scene + emissive markers. Keep look v5 contrast | Medium |
| 8 | Icons-on-charcoal-paper explainer for processes | 2.5D: planes with paper texture, SVG icons imported as curves, slow camera pan | Medium |
| 9 | Role labels in quotes + lower-left place/date tag | Add to the `title_card.py` family as `label_card()` and `place_date_tag()` | Easy |
| 10 | Chapter card over a live 3D frame from that chapter | `title_card.py`: allow a background render pass instead of a plain BG | Easy |
| 11 | ECU of hands/eyes for emotion | MakeHuman: hands and eyes hold up in ECU only with good skin SSS and eye shaders. Test 1 shot first | Medium |
| 12 | Single coloured rim light on dark (red = threat) | look v5 already does shadows/contrast. Add a "threat" light preset: one red rim, low fill. Keep real colours, no teal | Easy |
| 13 | Dense crowds behind shallow DOF | Instance 20–40 MakeHuman variants with geometry nodes. DOF hides repetition | Hard (render time) |
| 14 | Frozen bullet / blood macro | Particle-free: modelled drops + motion blur off | Medium |
| 15 | Behind-the-scenes Blender UI in the outro | Screen-record our own Blender session | Easy |
