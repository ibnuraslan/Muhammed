## Techniques observed

| Area | What Fern does |
|---|---|
| Characters | **Clay/statue figures**: grey, untextured, sculpted. Solves likeness, period costume and skin realism in one choice. Real people get framed archival photos |
| Hero object | Photoreal macro with slow reveal. The only fully textured thing in frame |
| Maps | Relief maps: a paper map displaced in 3D with tilt-shift DOF |
| Science | CT/X-ray = translucent cyan shader. Maths written out step by step. Orbits as a single white line |
| Labels | Small-caps serif labels in a cream box (name / role, year). Greek text glowing out of the bronze |
| Pace | Slow, contemplative: 6 s median, 20–45 s holds on diagrams |
| Book-ends | Opens and closes on the shipwreck |
