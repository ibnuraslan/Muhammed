## Techniques observed

| Area | What Fern does |
|---|---|
| Hook | Interview audio ("we kill the snitches") over a peaceful 3D macro, then a ritual in torchlight. Nickname tag |
| Voice | A real first-person witness carries most of the story. 3D re-enacts his exact words |
| Shot length | 3D median 4.3 s, but key testimony gets 30–62 s single shots (goat, oath, glasses) |
| Palette per world | KKK = torch orange on black. Bikers = amber bar + fluorescent green bathroom. Burnout = blue window. The Base = golden hour exteriors. Digital = phosphor green UI. Evidence = red line art on charcoal |
| Abstract facts | Location + venue + date + time tags. Red outline drawings for objects. Satellite map with red outline. Terminal dossier UI. Mock social-media UI. Sketch portraits for real people |
| Characters | Main character in 3D with a consistent face. Real criminals never in 3D likeness: sketches or blurred photos. Many faces lit from one side only |
| Aspect | 2.35:1 letterbox = "film", not "YouTube explainer" |
