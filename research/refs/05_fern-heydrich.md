## Techniques observed

| Area | What Fern does |
|---|---|
| Hook | Night sky + 2 parachutes + VO with one short sentence per shot. Target introduced on a desk of photos |
| Archival as props | Real photos printed and placed in 3D on wood, with handwritten captions. Maps are vintage paper with red lines and pinned photos |
| Villain | Heydrich is never a realistic 3D face: silhouette, back view, red-lit stage figure, or real archival. Heroes get realistic 3D faces |
| Red | Red light/neon = Nazi power and death (Blond Moses neon, red desk, red bed) |
| Dates | Typewriter-style date tags in a small cream box, centred low ("July 1941", "May 27, 1942") |
| POV | Binocular mask for the witness and surveillance |
| Violence | Implied: jammed gun, empty bed, hands, dots on a map |
