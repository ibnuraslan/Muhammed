Mary Celeste film, Part 3 (5:20-7:50) - asset credits

3D (all built in code here or in the shared library on claude/mary-celeste-3d)
  * Human bodies, rig and eyes: MakeHuman 1.x system data (CC0), via mary_celeste_3d/tools/mh_compile.py
    (base mesh, rig, eyes from the makehumancommunity/makehuman repository; targets.npz from the makehuman 1.3.2 wheel).
    UV masks for lips/nails/ears from the MPFB extension (CC0 data).
  * Textures and skies: Poly Haven (CC0) - dark_wooden_planks, weathered_planks, brown_planks_05, wood_floor_deck,
    kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky, kloofendal_38d_partly_cloudy_puresky, qwantani_dusk_2_puresky,
    overcast_soil_puresky, qwantani_sunset_puresky, kloofendal_overcast_puresky. https://polyhaven.com (CC0)
  * Elevation (Gibraltar, the Strait, Spanish and Moroccan coasts, the chart-table maquette): Terrain Tiles
    (Terrarium format) on the AWS Open Data Registry, https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
    Data sources and attribution: https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
    (includes SRTM, GMTED2010 and ETOPO1, courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey and NOAA; EU-DEM produced using
    Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union).
