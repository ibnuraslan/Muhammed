How it gets built

Real assets, not shapes

  • Windows and skies: real photographed 360° skies and views from Poly Haven, a free scanned-asset library
  • Furniture, plants, props: photo-scanned 3D models
  • Surfaces: scanned wood, fabric, concrete and asphalt
  • People: kept at medium or wide distance, with no hand or skin close-ups

Look (as in the Iceman film)

  • Soft natural light and a cool palette
  • Red used only on the map
  • Thin, tracked capital labels
  • Grain, bloom and vignette
  • Straight cuts, no wipes
