Look: same as the Iceman film

Light and colour

  • Soft, natural light. Blue-hour dawn, overcast daylight, clean interiors. No neon, no dark boxes.
  • Cool, desaturated steel palette like the Iceman glacier and lab, with one warm source per scene (a lamp, phone glow).
  • One graphic accent colour: the Iceman red, used only on the map.

Finish (same post chain as Iceman)

  • Rendered at 12 fps and interpolated to 24 fps with motion compensation
  • Bloom and halation, slight chromatic aberration, vignette, film grain
  • Floating dust in interiors, light drizzle in the street (3D-projected like the Iceman snow)
  • Straight cuts between shots. A short dissolve only where the set changes, and no wipes

Text (Iceman labels)

  • Thin, wide-tracked capitals with one small data line underneath, like “ÖTZTAL ALPS / Austrian–Italian border”
  • Used for names, the date and the map. Nothing else on screen.
  • “DRAMATIZATION” small in the corner on recreated scenes

Animation rules

  • No still frames. In every shot the camera moves and something in frame moves.
  • People are posed every frame from their skeleton: walking, typing, turning heads, placing objects.
  • Vehicles, ships and cranes follow animated paths; trees, flags and water move with wind and noise.
  • Fast close-ups (1–2s) cut against wides, as in the reference.

The four sets

Following the reference: a few complete environments, each used for several shots from different angles, instead of a new set for every line.

SET 1 · HOME

Living room, blue-hour dawn

Floor-to-ceiling windows onto wooded hills and a road below. Low sofa, coffee table, rug, floor lamp, bookshelf, a plant. He sits on the sofa in the black leather jacket.

SET 2 · BOARDROOM

Glass boardroom, overcast day

A long table, 7 people seated facing the table, the head chair empty. Through the glass: a realistic Santa Clara view of low-rise campus buildings, car parks, palm and oak trees, freeway traffic and the hills. Santa Clara has no skyscrapers, so no fake towers.

SET 3 · STREET

City street at dusk

A real four-lane road with cars, a bus and scooters moving, traffic lights, a crossing, wet pavement, pedestrians. Shop signs are proper lit panels with readable text, no floating glowing boxes. Alice walks the sidewalk.

SET 4 · PORT → MAP

Container port, daylight

A container ship at the quay, 3 gantry cranes, stacks, trucks, a tug with a wake, a second ship coming in. The camera rises out of the port into an Iceman-style relief map of East Asia, where China is highlighted.
