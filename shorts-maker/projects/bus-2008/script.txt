# The Greyhound bus killing (Manitoba, 2008) - no gore, creepy, factual.
# Sources: Wikipedia "Killing of Tim McLean", CBC, CTV, Global News (see research.md)

[01] July 30th, 2008. [02] A Greyhound bus is crossing the Canadian prairie.
[03] Twenty-two-year-old Tim McLean is heading home to Winnipeg.
[04] He puts on his headphones and falls asleep against the window.
[05] After a rest stop in Brandon, [06] a quiet man moves to the empty seat beside him.
[07] His name is Vince Li.
[08] Nobody notices the knife.
[09] Then the screaming starts.
[10] The driver pulls over, [11] and the passengers run off the bus into the fields.
[12] The driver and two men try to go back for Tim, [13] but they're driven off.
[14] So the driver disables the bus, [15] a trucker brings a hammer and a crowbar, [16] and they keep him trapped inside.
[17] For almost five hours, he stays in there with Tim.
[18] Police surround the bus.
[19] At 1:30 in the morning, he tries to escape through a window, [20] and police finally take him down.
[21] In court, doctors said he had untreated schizophrenia. [22] He believed God was telling him to do it.
[23] He was found not criminally responsible, [24] and in 2017, he was set completely free.
[25] Tim's mother said, "My son is still dead."
