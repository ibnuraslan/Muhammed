# Research: killing of Tim McLean (2008)

Verified facts used in the script (with sources):
- 30 Jul 2008, Greyhound bus 1170, Edmonton → Winnipeg, Trans-Canada Hwy ~30 km west of Portage la Prairie, Manitoba. [Wikipedia]
- Tim McLean, 22, carnival worker returning home to Winnipeg; sat near the back, asleep against the window with headphones. [Wikipedia]
- Vince Li boarded at Erickson (bus left 6:55 pm); moved next to McLean after the rest stop in Brandon. [Wikipedia]
- Driver stopped; passengers fled; the driver and two men tried to help and were driven off. [Wikipedia]
- RCMP got the report at 8:30 pm. The driver had engaged the emergency immobiliser; the driver, a passenger and a truck driver (who supplied a crowbar and hammer) kept Li from escaping. [Wikipedia]
- 1:30 am, 31 Jul: Li tried to leave by breaking a window; arrested with two Taser shots. [Wikipedia]
- 5 Mar 2009: not criminally responsible (schizophrenia); psychiatrist: he believed God told him to do it. [Wikipedia, IDS News, CTV]
- 10 Feb 2017: absolute discharge; now named Will (Lee) Baker. [CBC, Global News, Washington Times]
- Carol de Delley (mother): "…has faded back into society. My son is still dead." [Global News 2017]

Choices: no gore anywhere; Li is shown only as a silhouette (no caricature of a real, mentally ill person); the
attack is never shown, only reactions, sound and a censor badge. Generic bus livery (no Greyhound logo on screen).
