# v3: shot-by-shot copy of the reference (plan.md). Facts: Wikipedia "Killing of Tim McLean", CBC, CTV (../bus-2008/research.md)
[01] In 2008, a man on a Greyhound bus did something so horrifying, it shocked an entire country.
[02] Twenty-two-year-old Tim McLean was riding home to Winnipeg, when a stranger named Vince Li walked down the aisle, and took the empty seat right next to him. [03] Tim was exhausted. [04] He fell asleep against the window, [05] never noticing the knife Li was carrying.
[06] Then, without any warning, Li attacked.
[07] The driver slammed on the brakes, [08] and the screaming passengers ran off the bus. [09] A few men tried to go back in and help, but Li chased them away with the knife. [10] Then he turned back to Tim.
[11] Outside, [12] the passengers could only watch through the windows, [13] as Li did things [14] that none of them would ever forget. [15] For hours, he stayed in there with Tim.
[16] When the police finally arrived, Li tried to escape by smashing a window. [17] He hid between the seats, [18] until officers hit him with a taser.
[19] What police found on that bus haunted officers for years. [20] In court, Li was found not criminally responsible, because of schizophrenia. [21] And just nine years later, [22] he was set completely free.
[23] Tim McLean was only twenty-two. Should the man who did this ever have been released?
