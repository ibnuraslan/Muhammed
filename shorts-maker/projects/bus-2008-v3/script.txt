# Facts: Wikipedia "Killing of Tim McLean", CBC, CTV (see ../bus-2008/research.md)
[01] In 2008, a man sat down next to a sleeping stranger on a bus… [02] and what he did next shocked an entire country.
[03] Twenty-two-year-old Tim McLean was riding home to Winnipeg, [04] when a man named Vince Li got on.
[05] Li sat down right next to him. [06] Tim barely noticed, put his headphones on, and fell asleep.
[07] Then, without any warning, Li pulled out a large knife… [08] and attacked him.
[09] The driver slammed on the brakes, [10] and the passengers ran off the bus.
[11] A few men tried to go back and help, but Li chased them away.
[12] They trapped him inside… [13] and through the windows, the passengers watched.
[14] What they saw that night, they would never forget.
[15] For hours, he stayed in there with Tim.
[16] When police arrived, Li tried to escape by smashing a window.
[17] Officers hit him with a taser, and finally arrested him.
[18] What police found on that bus haunted officers for years.
[19] In court, Li was found not criminally responsible, because of schizophrenia… [20] and in 2017, he was set completely free.
[21] Tim McLean was only twenty-two. [22] Should the man who did this ever have been released?
